Structural drivers

Repair economics support total-loss frequency; unit supply also depends on claims

A higher fraction of claims can become total losses even while claim counts fall, and the reason is arithmetic: repair costs keep climbing while used-car values do not. We can track that spread monthly from public data and use it to nowcast total-loss frequency ahead of the quarterly industry release.
