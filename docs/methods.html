Provenance

Every number, where it came from, and what is wrong with it

This page exists so a reader can check us. Each collected dataset lists its source, how it was built, and its known gaps. Where we used licensed broker research we credit the figures and do not reproduce the documents.

Licensed research is credited, never reproduced

Some figures come from Barclays, J.P. Morgan and Freedom Broker notes and from a Bloomberg terminal export. Those documents are licensed to a named individual and are marked as not for reproduction or redistribution. We cite the figures and attribute them, and the files themselves are excluded from the public repository. Anything published here is either a public-domain government source, a company filing or disclosure, a press report, or our own calculation.

Hand-curated datasets

Neither Copart nor RB Global publishes a machine-readable panel of operating metrics such as unit growth by segment, average selling prices or cost per car. Those figures exist only in earnings-call transcripts and research notes, so they are transcribed by hand into data/manual. Every row carries a source column naming the document it came from, and derived rather than reported values are labelled as derived.

Source, method and known gaps, dataset by dataset

macro_cpi_long

monthly · 807 rows · collected 2026-09-18 00:29 UTC

Source. US Bureau of Labor Statistics public API (api.bls.gov), public domain

Method. Monthly seasonally-adjusted CPI index levels for vehicle repair, used vehicles, new vehicles, motor vehicle insurance, gasoline, transportation and all items. Year-over-year percent change computed as a 12-month change on the index level. Collected via v1 (keyless). Year-over-year values corrected on 18 September 2026 to match the same calendar month one year earlier, preserving missing months; original index levels and collection timestamp unchanged.

Gaps and caveats. The keyless v1 endpoint returns at most a 10-year window, so history before 2017 requires a free BLS_API_KEY. CPI measures consumer prices, which proxy but do not equal the insurer-paid repair costs that drive total-loss decisions. The Manheim wholesale index is not published by BLS and is hand-entered in data/manual instead.

research_allocation

Scenario · 9 rows · collected 2026-09-19 02:05 UTC

Source. Public carrier exposure dataset; analyst scenarios

Method. 100 × [starting Progressive weight × policy growth factor × retained allocation + remaining weight × Allstate policy growth factor]. Claims and TLF held flat.

Gaps and caveats. Weights and retained allocation are hypothetical; Allstate is only a proxy for the remaining book. Not an estimate of actual Copart volume.

research_carriers

Monthly/quarterly · 3 rows · collected 2026-09-19 02:05 UTC

Source. https://investor.progressive.com/financials/financial-news-releases/news-details/2026/Progressive-Reports-June-2026-Results/default.aspx; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/899051/000089905126000117/allcorp63026earningsreleas.htm; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/80661/000008066126000322/pgr202608ex99earningsrelea.htm

Method. Personal auto policies: Progressive agency plus direct; Allstate Protection auto. Compare June with June. August is an update, not a synchronized comparison.

Gaps and caveats. Two carriers only; policies are not claims or salvage units. Different products and territories; allocation to auctioneers is unknown.

research_earnings

Annual scenario · 4 rows · collected 2026-09-19 02:05 UTC

Source. https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/900075/000119312526387902/cprt-ex99_1.htm

Method. FY2026 consolidated revenue and costs. Service revenue scales by volume and RPU; vehicle revenue/cost scale by volume. Facility costs split fixed/variable with assumed 60% variable; other operating costs inflate. Reported tax rate and NCI adjustment; diluted shares adjusted by scenario.

Gaps and caveats. Fixed/variable split is assumed, not disclosed. Cost inflation bundles fuel and wages. No acquisition contributions, purchase accounting, capex, working capital or full cash-flow forecast. Scenarios are not price targets.

research_supply

Scenario · 9 rows · collected 2026-09-19 02:05 UTC

Source. Analyst sensitivity assumptions; not observed projections

Method. Exposure × claim frequency × total-loss frequency. Five years of compounded exposure and claims, endpoint TLF ratio. Base volume = 100.

Gaps and caveats. 22% starting TLF is a round scenario anchor, not a new measurement. Excludes market share, catastrophic spikes and non-insurance units. No probabilities assigned.

Method. Point-in-time (instant) facts such as cash, PP&E, equity and goodwill.

sec_quarterly

quarterly · 1321 rows · collected 2026-09-18 00:29 UTC

Source. SEC EDGAR XBRL company facts API (data.sec.gov), public domain

Method. Downloaded the companyfacts bundle for CPRT, RBA, ACVA and PGR. Kept duration facts of 60-120 days as quarters. Where an issuer files no Q4 10-Q, fiscal Q4 is derived as the 10-K full year minus the three reported quarters (flagged frame=derived_fy_minus_q1q2q3). Where the same period appears in several filings the most recently filed restated value is kept.

Gaps and caveats. Diluted EPS is not derived for Q4 because per-share figures do not sum. Copart stopped tagging GrossProfit and cost of revenue after FY2020, so gross margin for recent quarters comes from the hand-curated panel in data/manual/cprt_quarterly_financials.csv instead. Segment detail (US vs International, insurance vs non-insurance units, ASPs) is reported only as XBRL dimensions, which the companyfacts API strips out, so that also comes from the manual panel. RB Global's gross transaction value is not an XBRL concept at all. The most recent fiscal quarter appears only after the 10-Q or 10-K is filed, which lags the earnings release by days to weeks.

storm_county

cumulative over the period · 3730 rows · collected 2026-09-18 00:26 UTC

Source. NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information, Storm Events Database (public domain)

Method. Downloaded the yearly 'details' files for 2016-2026 and kept only event types that plausibly total a vehicle: hail, flood, flash flood, coastal flood, storm surge, hurricane, tornado and tropical storm. Heat, drought, cold and straight-line wind are excluded. Property damage strings such as '2.50K' and '1.2M' are parsed to dollars and summed.

Gaps and caveats. Storm Events counts REPORTED events, so densely populated counties are over-represented relative to sparsely populated ones. Property damage is an all-property figure, not vehicle-specific, so it proxies rather than measures salvage supply. County names come from NOAA's CZ_NAME field, which mixes counties and forecast zones, so joining to yard locations needs care. The current year is incomplete. Separately: NOAA's August 2026 seasonal outlook gives a 75 percent chance of a below-normal Atlantic hurricane season, so this data supports a claim about durable land advantage, not a forecast of catastrophe-driven upside this year.

tlf_backtest

quarterly · 45 rows · collected 2026-09-19 02:01 UTC

Source. Local CCC manual panel and BLS CPI; current dataset vintages.

Method. Expanding window with at least 12 training observations. Assume a one-quarter TLF publication delay: target Q trains through Q-2. CPI lags 1-3 only. Re-select lag using training error on identical training rows at each origin. Compare with trend-only and last available actual on identical target quarters.

Gaps and caveats. Pseudo-out-of-sample, not a real-time vintage backtest. CPI revisions and historical CCC publication dates are unavailable. Quarterly means can have missing months. Lag zero from the original in-sample search is excluded because target-quarter CPI would be unavailable at this forecast origin. Small test sample, overlapping training windows and mixed CCC definitions limit conclusions.

tlf_nowcast

quarterly · 35 rows · collected 2026-09-19 02:01 UTC

Source. Total-loss frequency from CCC Intelligent Solutions as cited in J.P. Morgan research (calendar 1Q19-3Q25) and by Copart on its FY26Q4 earnings call (calendar 2Q26). Repair and used-vehicle CPI from the US Bureau of Labor Statistics public API.

Method. Defined the repair-to-value 'scissors' as repair CPI year-over-year minus used-vehicle CPI year-over-year, averaged monthly CPI to calendar quarters, then fitted ordinary least squares of total-loss frequency on (a) the scissors lagged 3 quarter(s) and (b) a linear time trend that represents the structural drift from the ageing fleet and rising vehicle complexity. Lags 0-3 were tested and the highest adjusted R-squared chosen (R2=0.656, adjusted 0.629, n=28). Fitted: scissors +0.0618 pts per point of spread (p=0.0002), trend +0.396 pts per year (p=0.0006). The nowcast band is the 95 percent residual interval and is populated only for quarters after the last published CCC actual (2026-06-30).

Gaps and caveats. This fit selects lags in-sample; separate historical validation is in tlf_backtest. The band is plus/minus 1.96 residual standard deviations, not a calibrated forecast interval; it excludes parameter and lag-selection uncertainty and serial correlation. Only 28 overlapping quarterly observations, so this is directional rather than precise. Consumer CPI proxies but does not equal insurer-paid repair cost or wholesale salvage value. The relationship is correlational: fleet age, claim mix and deductible changes also move total-loss frequency. The CCC series cited by J.P. Morgan (percent of claims flagged total loss) and the annual CCC Crash Course figures use slightly different definitions.

Rules we hold ourselves to

  • No invented numbers. If a figure is not in a filing, a transcript, a public dataset or our own calculation from those, it does not appear.
  • Derived is labelled derived. Where we computed something the company did not report, such as RB Global's implied Q1 take rate, the table says so.
  • Scraped counts are listings, not sales. Inventory-tracker output is always described as listings observed, never as reported unit sales.
  • Models state their sample size. Every regression reports its number of observations, R-squared and confidence interval, and we say plainly when a sample is small.
  • Every chart has a table. No value is reachable only by hovering, which also keeps the site readable in print and for colour-vision-deficient readers.
  • Disconfirming evidence gets equal billing. The competitive data that supports the bear case is presented first on its own page, not buried.

Reproducing this site

  1. Clone the repository and create the virtual environment.
  2. Copy .env.example to .env and set at least SEC_USER_AGENT to your name and email, which the SEC requires.
  3. Run python -m src.collect.sec_financials and python -m src.collect.macro.
  4. Run python -m src.build.tlf_nowcast.
  5. Run python -m src.site.build_site and open docs/index.html.

The full step-by-step version, written for someone who has never used an API key, is in SETUP.md in the repository.
