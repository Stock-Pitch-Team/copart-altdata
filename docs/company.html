What the filings actually show

Revenue is flat, margin is compressed, and the cause is visible: cost per unit is climbing while units fall. Every figure on this page is either from an SEC filing or from company disclosure on the earnings call.

Not investment advice. This site is student work prepared for a stock-pitch competition. Nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell any security. The authors hold no position and are not licensed advisers.

Every figure is sourced. Where a number came from a licensed broker report, the report is credited but not reproduced.
