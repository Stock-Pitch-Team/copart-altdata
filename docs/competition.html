Competitive position

IAA won volume. Look at what it paid.

The bear case is that Copart is structurally losing share to IAA. The evidence for volume loss is real and we show it first. What the volume charts leave out is price: RB Global's take rate fell 110 basis points while it was winning, and the company itself attributes that to volume-linked pricing incentives.
