What would establish an advantage?

Copart needs to generate higher net seller recoveries and dependable selling speed on comparable cars, and turn its footprint into profitable throughput. Rising repair costs alone cannot establish those advantages. The existing TLF model still loses to the last-observation baseline in historical validation.

Change the assumptions

These are sensitivities, not forecasts. Supply uses a starting TLF of 22% and five years; earnings uses the FY2026 consolidated baseline, 60% variable facility cost, unchanged interest and shares.

Industry supply is separate from Copart volume: market share and carrier allocation must connect them. Acquisition effects are excluded; fuel and wages are bundled into inflation because their cost shares are not disclosed.

Import and reproduce

Copy the header-only templates in data/templates; fill them with public or authorized data and retain source URLs. Run python -m src.analysis.imports sales path/to/sales.csv (replace sales with inventory, manifests, yards or storms). Each import replaces that local dataset, so inventory imports must include accumulated history. Then run python run_all.py --site.

Raw imports stay in ignored data/private; only aggregate diagnostics enter the site. No sample auction results are published. Read the schema and research guide before importing.
